Why FrontDoor

A Head Start For New Practices

Opening a practice means a long list of firsts. Your website shouldn't be one more thing to learn. Here's what changes when we build it for you.

01

Look Established From Day One

A clear, professional site helps a new name feel trustworthy enough to reach out to. Your photo and credentials go up front, with one obvious way to book.

 02

Easy To Find

Every page carries its own title, description and schema markup, so your name, address, hours and services reach search engines and AI assistants in a form they read. A blog comes with it, first post already written.

 03

Compliance Built In

Accessibility and privacy are handled before your site goes live, not bolted on afterwards. It is tested against WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard ADA claims are measured against. Nothing tracks your visitors, no form asks for health details, and the 988 crisis line sits in every footer.
Our mission

Care Shouldn't Wait On A Website

We take the website off your plate, so the sooner your practice looks real and feels easy to reach, the sooner the people who need you can find their way in. That is the whole reason we exist.

01

For Practitioners Starting Out

Most new practices begin with one person, a license and a lot of nerve. We give that person a professional presence from day one, without an agency's cost or a builder's learning curve.

02

For The People Who Need Care

A clear, calm website makes it a little easier to take the first step and reach out. When good practices are simple to find and to trust, more people get the help they were looking for.

03

For The Field As A Whole

Small, independent practices are a big part of how behavioral health reaches communities. By helping more of them get established and stay visible, we strengthen the whole field.

How it works

Four Steps, Zero Stress

You choose a design and answer a few questions. We do everything else, and walk you through the result before it goes live.

  1. 01

    Pick A Design

    Browse designs made for your practice area and size. Not sure which fits? We'll help you choose.

  2. 02

    Tell Us About Your Practice

    Fill out a short questionnaire about your services, your approach and the look you love. That's the main work on your plate.

  3. 03

    We Build It For You

    We design the pages, write the copy, source imagery and set up hosting, your domain and business email, all around your brand.

  4. 04

    Review & Go Live

    We present your site on a one-to-one call, make your tweaks on the spot, then publish it.
