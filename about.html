About FrontDoor

Built For Behavioral Health Practices

FrontDoor designs, writes and looks after websites for therapists, counselors, psychiatrists, group practices, clinics and programs. We are a service of iTrust Insights.

What We Do

You pick one of our designs and answer a short questionnaire about your practice. We handle the rest: the pages, the copy, the imagery, hosting, your domain and your business email, all built around your brand.

Before anything goes live, we walk you through the finished site on a call and make your changes there. You also get a simple editor for changing text and images yourself.

Visitors to a practice website are often deciding whether to reach out at all. Each design is built for that moment: a calm page, your photo and credentials up front, and one obvious way to book.

Every Design

What You Can Count On

  1. 01

    You Own Your Site

    The domain is registered to you and the content is yours. We host it and look after it for you.

  2. 02

    Privacy Minded

    No contact form on any design asks for health information. If you want online intake forms that collect health details, those need a HIPAA-compliant tool that will sign a Business Associate Agreement. We flag that with you before launch.

  3. 03

    Tested For Accessibility

    Every design is tested for color contrast, keyboard navigation and heading structure against WCAG 2.1 AA, and fixed where it falls short.

  4. 04

    A Crisis Line In Every Footer

    Every design carries the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in its footer, so the number is there for anyone who lands on the page.
