Privacy Policy

FrontDoor is a service of iTrust Insights. Here is what we collect when you use this site or ask us about a website, what we do with it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated November 12, 2026

What We Collect

When you fill in the request form on this site, we receive what you type into it:

  • Your name and email address
  • The design you like, if you chose one
  • Your answers to a few short questions about your practice, such as whether you are new and when you want your website live
  • Anything you write in the message box

Like any website, the companies that host and deliver this site can see technical details of your visit, such as your IP address and the browser you use. We do not use that information to identify you.

Please Do Not Send Health Information

This form is for practice owners asking about a website. It is not a place for client or patient information, and we do not want it. Please leave out names, conditions, diagnoses or anything else about a client or about your own health.

If you are looking for care, this is not the right place to ask. If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency.

How We Use It

We use your details to reply to your request, suggest designs that fit your practice, and follow up about building your website. We use a trusted service to deliver form messages to us. We do not sell your details, and we do not share them for advertising.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your request for as long as we need it to reply and follow up. If we do not end up working together, we delete it when it is no longer useful, or sooner if you ask.

Your Choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, the law may give you more rights than that, and we will honor them.

To make a request, use the request form on our home page and put “privacy request” at the start of your message. We will reply by email.

Websites We Build For Practices

This policy covers the FrontDoor website only. A website we build for a practice belongs to that practice, and the practice is responsible for how it handles what its visitors send. The sample designs in our gallery are demonstrations, so please do not enter real personal information into them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
